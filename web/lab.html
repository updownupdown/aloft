Try the songbirds in different voices. Pick a voice, a pitch and a space, play a few songs, and keep the one that sounds most like a real bird. The game uses whatever you keep.

Voice

Whistle is how the birds sounded before. Natural uses short, fast sweeps, the way small birds really sing. Two-voice syrinx adds the second, quieter line songbirds sing at the same time. Breathy is softer and airier, like a bird heard through leaves.

Pitch

Real songbirds sing high, mostly between 2 and 8 kHz. The songs keep their notes; only the octave changes. The owl always stays low.
